[B]Attention, Sailors![/B]

Tired of your classic ship? Need more space on this tin can? Or perhaps you've overdone it with new recruits, and they're drowning at the slightest flooding? Well then, you've come to the right place! Customize the ship to your twisted taste with this [B]balanced[/B] mod!

This mod allows you to (just imagine) craft, rearrange, and even place your [B]OWN[/B] DIY items! (which are practically identical to vanilla items!). Moreover, these items even have a purchasable version in case you're not too fond of the shine of homemade creations.

[IMG]https://i.ibb.co/nz8Fk5z/Untitled-4.png[/IMG]

[B]So, what items are we talking about? Almost everything![/B]
Various terminals, pumps, cabinets, fabricators...

Download this mod now, and we promise you'll discover new depths in the already deep gameplay of Barotrauma!

[B]What's new?[/B] (Update "ItWillSinkInLUA")
Well, new items have been added. (like chairs, for example)
Some old items are hidden. (such as mechanic's and engineer's chests)
And generally, there are so many small changes that I'm too lazy to list them all. All for the better, I hope.

All items (including their variations, a whopping 70 of them!) have been updated according to the developers' innovations. I've automated this process, so falling behind will no longer be an issue.

Now the mod uses LUA. ?!?1!? (yes)
[B]Both the SERVER[/B] and [B]CLIENT parts[/B] are [B]REQUIRED[/B]!

So, why LUA?
To [B]make things worse for everyone[/B] improve the balance and make it more vanilla.
Yes, this mod is about campaign and balance, just a reminder. (For something else, there's [URL=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2764140582][B][I]BaroCraftables+[/I][/B][/URL])

Now each item added by the mod has a maximum quantity restriction on the boat.
[IMG]https://i.ibb.co/3pnpW97/sdfsdf.png[/IMG]
Everyone has a base install limit.
Books raise it from there. There are four, one per field: supplies, electrical, machinery, interior.
The first book of a field doubles the limit for all of its items and unlocks their crafting. The second one triples it. A third does nothing - sell it back.
Books are single-use. They are sold in shops and occasionally turn up in wrecks and abandoned outposts.
The knowledge goes to the whole crew at once and is written into the save. Anyone who joins later gets the same limit, no need to read it again.
The mod no longer overrides vanilla talents.
